About Trackly

We built the tracker Google was never going to be.

Trackly exists to solve one problem: students refreshing ICAI, ICSI, UPSC, SSC, and IBPS websites, over and over, waiting for an official update that hasn't happened yet.

Our mission

A student should be able to subscribe to an update once — a result, an admit card, a correction window — and move on with their day. When the official body actually releases it, Trackly tells them. That's the entire product.

How Trackly is different from Google

Google is built to search the whole internet, every time you ask it something. Trackly is built to watch a small, specific list of official pages on your behalf, so you only have to ask once. Google is for searching. Trackly is for tracking.

Our commitment to official information

  • We never predict exam dates or results.
  • We never estimate a release window.
  • If information hasn't been officially released, we say so clearly — not vaguely.
  • Every update we surface will link back to its original official source.
