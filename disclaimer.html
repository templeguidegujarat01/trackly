Trackly does not create official announcements

Trackly does not issue, author, or influence any exam schedule, result, notification, or circular. Every update shown on Trackly originates from the official institute itself.

Trackly only tracks publicly available official information

Trackly's role is limited to watching official sources and telling you when something on them changes. It does not estimate, predict, or guess dates or outcomes under any circumstance.

Always verify critical information

For anything time-sensitive or consequential — an exam date, a result, an admit card — always confirm directly on the relevant institute's official website before acting on it. Trackly is a convenience layer, not a replacement for the official source.
