How It Works

Five steps. Then you stop checking.

Here's the exact flow Trackly is built around, from choosing an exam to getting told it's live.

  1. 1

    Choose an institute

    Start with ICAI, ICSI, UPSC, SSC, or IBPS — whichever body runs your exam.

  2. 2

    Choose your course

    Narrow it down: Foundation or Final for ICAI, Civil Services or NDA for UPSC, and so on.

  3. 3

    Choose the update type

    Pick exactly what you're waiting on — Result, Admit Card, Registration, Syllabus, or another tracker type.

  4. 4

    Subscribe

    Confirm your subscription once. No repeated searching after this.

  5. 5

    Get notified

    The moment the official body releases it, Trackly lets you know.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about tracking

How will I be notified?

Notifications aren't live in this version yet. This page describes the full experience Trackly is being built toward — email notifications are planned for an upcoming phase.

What if the official update never comes on the date I expect?

You'll keep seeing 'Not officially released yet' for as long as that's true. Trackly never guesses a date on your behalf.

Which institutes can I track right now?

ICAI, ICSI, UPSC, SSC, and IBPS are supported in Version 1. More may be added later through configuration.

Does Trackly cost anything?

No. Tracking updates is free in Version 1.
