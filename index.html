Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about Trackly

Is Trackly official?

No. Trackly is an independent tracking tool. It is not affiliated with ICAI, ICSI, UPSC, SSC, IBPS, or any government body.

Does Trackly replace official websites?

No. Trackly points you back to the official source for every update — always verify anything critical there directly.

Is it free?

Yes. Tracking updates is free in Version 1.

How are updates tracked?

Trackly is built to watch each official source and mark every update as Tracking, Pending, or Released. The tracking engine is rolling out in an upcoming phase.

Can I track multiple institutes?

Yes. You'll be able to track topics across ICAI, ICSI, UPSC, SSC, and IBPS at once, from a single subscription list.

Do I need an account?

Not to browse. An account will be needed once subscriptions and notifications launch, so Trackly knows where to send them.

Will Trackly send spam?

No. You'll only be notified about updates you specifically subscribed to — nothing else, ever.

Which institutes are supported?

ICAI, ICSI, UPSC, SSC, and IBPS in Version 1. More may be added later.
