Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about tracking institutes

Can I track multiple institutes?

Yes. You can track topics across ICAI, ICSI, UPSC, SSC, and IBPS at the same time, all from one list.

Does Trackly replace official websites?

No. Trackly points you back to the official source for every update — always verify anything critical there directly.

Which updates are monitored?

Results, exam timetables, admit cards, notifications, circulars, and other official update types specific to each institute — see each institute's card above.

How often are official sources checked?

That depends on the tracking engine, which hasn't launched yet. This page will be updated with the real answer once it's live — we won't guess in the meantime.

Can I change my institute later?

Yes. You'll be able to track updates from as many or as few institutes as you like, and adjust anytime.

Is Trackly free?

Yes. Tracking updates is free in Version 1.
