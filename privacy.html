Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

This policy describes how Trackly handles information in its current version. Trackly does not yet have accounts, a backend, or a notification system — this policy will be revised as those features are introduced, and this page will always reflect what's actually true at the time.

Information we collect

In this version, Trackly does not ask you to create an account or submit personal information. Standard technical information (such as browser type and page requests) may be logged by our hosting provider, as is normal for any website.

How information will be used in the future

When subscriptions and notifications launch, Trackly will ask for an email address so it can tell you when an official update is released. That email will be used only to send the updates you asked for — never sold, and never shared with advertisers.

Cookies

Trackly does not currently use tracking or advertising cookies.

Third-party official sources

Trackly links to official institute websites (ICAI, ICSI, UPSC, SSC, IBPS). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those external sites.

Changes to this policy

We'll update this page whenever what Trackly actually does changes, and update the "last updated" date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to support@tracklyapp.com.
